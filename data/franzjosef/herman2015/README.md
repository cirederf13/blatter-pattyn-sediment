Data from the analysis of Herman et al. (2015, Science).

- `ln_edot.txt`, `sigma_ln_edot.txt`: mean and standard deviation of ln(erosion rate, m/yr) in 700 m bins of distance to the sampling station (15 bins over 10.5 km, the first two NaN), from the 14 samples that are not from storms.
- `glacierwidth.txt`, `glacierwidthLENGTH.txt`: glacier width (m) against distance to the station (m, 10 m steps).

The erosion is the suspended load only (no bed-load factor), with a rock density of 2700 kg/m3.
